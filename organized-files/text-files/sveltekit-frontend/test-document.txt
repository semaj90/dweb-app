DEED OF CONVEYANCE

This Deed of Conveyance is made this 15th day of August, 2025, between John Smith (the "Grantor"), and Jane Doe (the "Grantee").

WITNESSETH: That the Grantor, for and in consideration of the sum of $350,000, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, does hereby grant, bargain, sell, and convey unto the Grantee all right, title, and interest in and to the following described real property:

LEGAL DESCRIPTION: Lot 15, Block 3, Springfield Estates Subdivision, as recorded in Plat Book 12, Page 45, in the office of the County Recorder of Deeds, Hamilton County, Ohio.

PROPERTY ADDRESS: 123 Main Street, Springfield, Ohio 45504

The property is conveyed subject to:
1. All existing easements and rights of way
2. Current property taxes and assessments
3. Zoning ordinances and building restrictions
4. Any covenants and restrictions of record

The Grantor hereby covenants with the Grantee that the Grantor is lawfully seized of said premises in fee simple and has good right to sell and convey the same to the Grantee; that the same are free from all encumbrances except as herein stated; and that the Grantor will warrant and defend the same to the Grantee against all lawful claims and demands.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Grantor has executed this deed on the day and year first above written.
